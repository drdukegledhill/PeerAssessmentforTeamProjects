Peer Assessment Simulator v1.1

Enter your team members' names and experiment with ratings to see how normalisation works in real time.

Set up your team

Enter the names of your team members - do not include yourself. You are the rater here, not the rated.

Separate names with commas. Enter at least 2 people.

Simulation only - not your actual scores. This tool processes only your ratings. In the real assessment, each person's score is the average of ratings from all their peers, and normalisation is applied to those averaged scores. What this simulator shows is: if everyone on your team rated exactly as you are rating here, these would be the resulting normalised scores. Use it to understand how normalisation works - not to predict your final result.

DNA simplification: In this simulator, rating someone 0 immediately flags them as Did Not Attend (DNA). In the real tool, DNA is only triggered when every peer gives a score of 0 — a single 0 from one rater is not enough.

Things to try

  • Give everyone the same rating (e.g. all 7s) - notice that everyone still ends up with 5. Inflation cancels out completely.
  • Slide one person to 0 - watch them get flagged as DNA and removed from the group median. See how the remaining scores shift.
  • Give one person a much lower rating - see how it drags the group median down, and therefore lifts everyone else's score.
  • Try a wide spread (e.g. 2, 5, 7, 9) versus a narrow spread (e.g. 4, 5, 6) - the adjustment will be the same but the spread of outcomes differs.
  • Give someone a 1 or 2 - watch the normalised score respond when the group median is high.
